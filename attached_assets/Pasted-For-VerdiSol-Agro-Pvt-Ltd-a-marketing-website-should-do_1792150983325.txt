## 3. The marketing scheme for VerdiSol Agro

A marketing scheme is the plan explaining how you will reach customers, promote your services and products, generate enquiries, and convert those enquiries into business.

I recommend the following approach.

Phase 1 — Brand awareness

Establish a professional online presence using your website, Facebook, LinkedIn and WhatsApp Business. Publish agricultural content using your green, gold and white brand colours.

Phase 2 — Attract potential customers

Optimise your website for searches related to your actual services in Zimbabwe. Publish useful articles, service descriptions, project photographs and agricultural advice.

Phase 3 — Generate leads

Add buttons such as “Request a Quote”, “Talk to an Expert” and “Enquire on WhatsApp”. Collect only the information needed to respond to prospective customers.

Phase 4 — Convert and retain customers

Respond to enquiries promptly, prepare quotations, follow up with interested customers and encourage repeat business through useful updates and professional service.

## 5. What should be measured?

Track the following marketing indicators every month:

- Website visitors and traffic sources.
- Google search impressions and clicks.
- WhatsApp clicks and contact-form submissions.
- Number of qualified enquiries.
- Quotations issued and sales won.
- Marketing expenditure compared with revenue generated.

You can use Google Analytics and Google Search Console to measure website performance and search visibility.
